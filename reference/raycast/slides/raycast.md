# Basic content slide

The base typography uses **Inter** with `font-feature-settings: ss03` for the signature alternate single-story g. White is the primary action color, *mono labels* take uppercase tracking, and the dark canvas is the only surface.

- Body copy stays at **16pt** on near-black `#07080a`.
- Depth comes from a **four-step surface ladder** (`#07080a → #0d0d0d → #101111 → #121212`).
- The brand rejects: drop shadows, gradients on chrome, pill buttons for body text.
