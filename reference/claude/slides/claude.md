# 01

## On craft — what serif typography asks of a deck.

---

# Basic content slide

The base typography rests on a **serif headline** (Copernicus → Fraunces fallback) and a **sans body** (StyreneB → Inter). Inline `code` uses a warm cream wash, and *italic* spans serve as the magazine-style emphasis.

- Body copy stays at **17pt** with 1.65 leading for editorial readability.
- Headings carry deliberate negative letter-spacing (-0.025em on h1).
- Numbers come in as **italic serif numerals**, never bold sans.

# A workspace that reads like prose.

<div class="spread">
<div class="col-body">

The promise of an editorial interface is that ideas land at their natural pace. You don't have to fight the screen for breathing room, and your reader doesn't have to decode a chart to find the point you cared about.

That means giving each thought a paragraph, each paragraph a column, and each column a margin you can write into. The serif handles the cadence; the system handles the geometry.

</div>
<div class="col-aside">

### Editor's note

> Cream is the canvas. Coral is the cue. Everything else stays out of the way.
