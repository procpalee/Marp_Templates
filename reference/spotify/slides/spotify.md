# Basic content slide

The base typography stays **bold sans** on a near-black canvas. *Pill badges* mark genre or category, and inline `code` glows in brand green.

- Body copy stays at **16pt** for high-density screens.
- Uppercase labels carry `0.14em` letter-spacing for that "label voice".
- Numbers and metadata live in mono for clarity.
