# Basic content slide

The base typography uses **Airbnb Cereal VF** (Inter fallback) with weight 400 body and 700 display. *Mono labels* take small uppercase tracking, and inline `code` is rare — the brand trusts photography and generous whitespace over typographic muscle.

- Body copy stays at **16pt** with 1.5 line-height.
- The single brand voltage is **Rausch `#ff385c`** — primary CTAs, the search orb, the rating dot.
- Cards use 14px radius and 16px gutters (tighter than SaaS marketing).
