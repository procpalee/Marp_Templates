# Basic content slide

The base typography uses **figmaSans** (Inter fallback) with weight 700 headlines and weight 400 body. Inline `code` rests on lime, and *mono labels* take uppercase tracking.

- Body copy stays at **17pt** with 1.45 line-height (tight reading rhythm).
- Hierarchy comes from **weight** alone — never opacity.
- Color blocks substitute traditional elevation. No shadows on panels.
