# Basic content slide

The base typography uses **SF Pro Display** for headings (Inter fallback) and **SF Pro Text** for body. Inline `code` is rare; the system prefers prose with a single Action Blue link.

- Body copy stays at **17pt** — editorial-paced reading rhythm (not 16).
- Headlines carry deliberate negative letter-spacing for the signature "Apple tight" cadence.
- Hierarchy comes from **weight and size**, never decorative color.

# This is how a product is born.

## Years of research. Hundreds of prototypes. One thing that feels like nothing else.

We started with a single question — what would it feel like if the device disappeared, and only the music remained? Every decision after that one was about removing friction: every gram, every cable, every menu, every micro-second of latency.

The result is a product you don't think about while you use it. And, hopefully, one you remember long after.

# Built to last.

## And built to be passed on.

Every Symphony ships with a multi-year warranty, parts you can replace yourself, and a trade-in program that takes the old one back at full value. The product is the point. The packaging is recyclable. The story doesn't have to end with you.
