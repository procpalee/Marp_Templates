# Basic content slide

The base typography uses **Universal Sans Display** for headings (Inter/Arial fallback) and **Universal Sans Text** for body. *Mono labels* take small uppercase tracking. The single chromatic color is **Electric Blue `#3E6AE1`** — reserved for primary CTAs and the occasional link.

- Body copy stays at **16pt** with 1.5 line-height.
- The system is deliberately monochrome plus one blue. No semantic colors.
- Depth comes from photography and whitespace — no shadows anywhere.
